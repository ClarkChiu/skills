# Layouts — the page-role catalog

Seven roles. A card set is assembled from these, not from a fixed template. Each role
lists its job, structure, title cap, the ratios it suits, and the common failure.
Character caps assume Chinese; Latin can run ~1.7× longer.

## 1. Cover

- **Job:** earn the tap. One hook, one strong visual.
- **Structure:** big display title (`--h-hero`) + one visual (supplied image / screenshot
  / object) + an optional bottom strip of 3–5 key points or an issue label.
- **Title cap:** ≤ 14 Chinese chars, ≤ 2 lines. Put nuance in later cards.
- **Suits:** all ratios. On 1.91:1 keep the title center-left in a clear band.
- **Common fail:** hollow center — enlarge title or visual, or add the bottom strip.

## 2. Point (single statement)

- **Job:** land one idea.
- **Structure:** a title (`--h-xl`) + a lead sentence + 0–3 short fragments. Lots of air.
- **Title cap:** 12–24 Chinese chars.
- **Suits:** the workhorse of carousels (4:5, 1:1, document).
- **Common fail:** two ideas crammed in — split into two Point cards.

## 3. List / checklist

- **Job:** an enumerable set (steps, tips, do/don't).
- **Structure:** a short title + 3–6 rows, each a one-line fragment with a marker
  (number, check, hairline). Equal vertical rhythm.
- **Title cap:** 8–16 Chinese chars.
- **Suits:** 4:5, 1:1, document carousel.
- **Common fail:** > 6 rows → the rows shrink. Split across two cards instead.

## 4. Comparison

- **Job:** A vs B, before/after, myth/reality.
- **Structure:** two columns or two stacked bands sharing a hairline; a label per side +
  2–4 contrast rows.
- **Title cap:** 8–16 Chinese chars.
- **Suits:** 4:5, 1:1.
- **Common fail:** lopsided columns — balance the row count, or it reads as one-sided.

## 5. Quote / pull

- **Job:** a breathing beat; a quotable line that sets tone.
- **Structure:** one large quote (`--h-xl`/serif), an attribution, generous negative
  space; on Editorial, let the texture/accent show stronger here.
- **Title cap:** the quote ≤ 3 lines.
- **Suits:** 4:5, 1:1, 9:16.
- **Common fail:** treating it like a content card — a quote card should feel sparse.

## 6. Screenshot frame

- **Job:** show evidence (a UI, a chart, a result) cleanly.
- **Structure:** a clean target-ratio frame on plain/paper background, the screenshot
  placed with safe padding, a short caption. Follow `screenshot-treatment.md` for
  object-fit and cropping.
- **Title cap:** caption ≤ 1 line; optional short title 8–14 chars.
- **Suits:** 4:5, 1:1. Give the screenshot 55–70% of the canvas; cut side text if cramped.
- **Common fail:** screenshot too small — reduce surrounding copy, not the image.

## 7. Summary / CTA

- **Job:** close the set; recap or single call to action.
- **Structure:** a compact recap (3–5 points or a one-line takeaway) + an optional CTA
  line (save / follow / link-in-bio). No new ideas.
- **Title cap:** 8–16 Chinese chars.
- **Suits:** last card of any carousel; the FB single card is often a Cover+Summary hybrid.
- **Common fail:** introducing a new argument on the closer — keep it a recap.

## Assembling a set

A typical IG 4:5 carousel: **Cover → 3–6 Point/List/Comparison cards → optional Quote
beat → Summary**. Keep it to a coherent arc; if the content needs > ~8 content cards,
it is two posts, not one.
